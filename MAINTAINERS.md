## Becoming a Maintainer

New maintainers are nominated by existing maintainers and approved by lazy
consensus (no objection within 7 days). Candidates should have:

- Sustained contributions over 3+ months
- Demonstrated understanding of the project architecture
- Commitment to the project's long-term direction

## Emeritus Maintainers

Maintainers who are no longer active may move to emeritus status. They retain
recognition for their contributions but no longer have merge authority.
