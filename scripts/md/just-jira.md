You don't need a ticket system running on someone else's server
to remember what you were doing yesterday.

git-aint tracks work in markdown files that live next to your code.
No sprints. No story points. No JQL. No board with seventeen columns
that nobody agreed on. No second system that drifts out of sync
the moment someone forgets to update it.

Your tasks are files. Your history is git log. Your backlog is `ls`.
If that sounds too simple, you might be overcomplicating it.
